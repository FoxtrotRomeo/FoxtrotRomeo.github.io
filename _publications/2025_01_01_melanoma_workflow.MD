Melanoma is the most common form of skin cancer and is responsible for thousands of deaths each year. New therapies have arrived, but metastasis remains the main driver of both mortality and loss of quality of life — and models for predicting it have generally drawn on a single data source at a time.

We compare a range of unimodal and multimodal models on a cohort derived from electronic health records, with the practical aim of helping clinicians direct attention toward the patients at highest risk, where an earlier diagnosis is most likely to change the outcome. Alongside the results, the paper sets out the workflow itself so that it can be reused on other cohorts.

This was the direct predecessor to the multicancer work published in *JCO Clinical Cancer Informatics*.
