I am a PhD student at the [Department of Computer and Systems Sciences](https://www.su.se/english/divisions/department-of-computer-and-systems-sciences) at Stockholm University, where I am part of the [Data Science Research Group](https://www.su.se/english/research/research-catalogue/research-groups/6/data-science-research-group). My supervisors are Professor [Panagiotis Papapetrou](https://www.su.se/profiles/ppapa-1.194080) and Senior Lecturer [Ioanna Miliou](https://www.su.se/profiles/iomi2003-1.548427).

My work sits at the intersection of machine learning and health. I am interested in three things in particular: predicting disease progression from electronic health records, developing explainability methods that make models legible to the clinicians who have to act on them, and modelling dyadic interaction in psychotherapy — how the exchange between therapist and patient unfolds, and what it tells us about the working alliance between them.

The thread running through all of it is multimodal data. Clinical notes, structured records, audio, gaze, facial movement: each captures something the others miss, and I am interested in how to combine them and in what happens at the seams where they meet.

My doctoral project, [Let's talk about nonverbal communication](https://www.su.se/forskning/forskningsprojekt/l%C3%A5t-oss-prata-om-icke-verbal-kommunikation), is a collaboration with the Department of Psychology at Stockholm University, funded by the Marcus and Amalia Wallenberg Foundation.

I came to this from clinical practice rather than from computer science. I trained as a physiotherapist in Padua and spent a year as the sole physiotherapist for lung transplant recipients at the Azienda Ospedale Università Padova, following patients from post-operative intensive care through to discharge. That experience is still the thing that shapes how I think about clinical machine learning: a model is only useful if the person standing at the bedside can understand what it is telling them and why.

You can find my papers on the [publications page](/publications/), and reach me at [franco.rugolon@dsv.su.se](mailto:franco.rugolon@dsv.su.se).---
