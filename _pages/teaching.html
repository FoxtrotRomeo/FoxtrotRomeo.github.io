--- permalink: /teaching/ title: "Teaching" author_profile: true --- In the Fall semester I give a lecture on Data Modalities and run the laboratory sessions for *Foundations of Data Science*. ## Master's thesis supervision I am available to supervise Master's theses in the Spring semester. The topics I propose usually fall into one of three areas: - machine learning applied to dyadic interactions in psychotherapy - cancer development and progression - data mining from electronic health records I am happy to consider projects outside these areas as well, provided you bring a genuine interest in the topic and the commitment to see it through. Two of the projects below have since been published as peer-reviewed papers with the student as first author, and I will always encourage a strong thesis toward publication. ## Supervised and co-supervised theses - **Evaluating Explainability Techniques for Machine Learning in Healthcare** — Katja Wilde.
