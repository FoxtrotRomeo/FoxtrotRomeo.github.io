--- permalink: /teaching/ title: "Teaching" author_profile: true --- In the Fall semester I give a lecture on Data Modalities and run the laboratory sessions within the *Foundations of Data Science* course. ## Master's thesis supervision I am available to supervise Master's theses in the Spring semester. The topics I propose usually fall into one of three areas: - machine learning applied to dyadic interactions in psychotherapy - cancer development and progression - data mining from electronic health records I am happy to consider projects outside these areas as well, provided you bring a genuine interest in the topic and the commitment to see it through. ## Previously supervised and co-supervised theses - Predicting the Onset of Metastasis by Multimodal Machine Learning in Lung Cancer and Melanoma Patients - Evaluating Explainability Techniques for Machine Learning in Healthcare - Predicting Chronic Kidney Disease in Jalisco: An XAI-Enhanced Multimodal Approach with Geospatial and Demographic Data - Predicting Symptom Change in Functional Somatic Syndromes through Multimodal Machine Learning and Explainability - Integrating Genetics and Epigenetics for Predicting the Mutational Landscape in Lung Adenocarcinoma
